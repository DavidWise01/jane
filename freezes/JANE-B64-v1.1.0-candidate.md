## Historical authorship referent
Enheduanna, circa 2300 BCE, is conventionally identified as the earliest known named author associated with surviving literature. Attribution of specific works remains academically debated. This is a referent for authorship *method*, not a transfer of authorship or copyright to contemporary authors.

## Four-register B64 learning cycle
- a(self): schedules and selects a source or causal action without changing the source.
- b([i]): predicts what evidence, authorship or relationships are expected before retrieval.
- c([id]): retrieves and checks actual public material; records success, errors or UNKNOWN.
- d[(sid)]: appends original source provenance, prediction, observation, mismatch and revised hypotheses.

`observation != prediction != inference != authorship`.
Learning updates a hypothesis only from observed accessible evidence and preserves the unaltered original. No quorum.
