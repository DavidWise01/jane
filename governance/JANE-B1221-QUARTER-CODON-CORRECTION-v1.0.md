## Biological terminology boundary

A genome is a long collection of nucleotide sequences; a whole genome is not one 64-position codon. The project uses "codon" as a **logical 64-address block**, not a biological genome, actual nucleotide sequence, quantum entanglement, or a physical quantum-dot device.
