## Invariant
An unusual observation is not automatically false, hostile, or disposable. It is retained as a separate evidence record with its uncertainty status. An unverified anomaly may inform caution but cannot silently overwrite a baseline.

For an estimated baseline bias b and residual e = observed - predicted, define a robust clipped update:
b_next = (1-alpha)*b + alpha*clip(e, -tau, +tau).
The raw e and the clipped update MUST both be retained. The threshold tau is a configurable policy parameter, not a measured confidence interval. Anomalous if |e| > tau. An anomaly classification is not an assertion that the underlying observation is invalid.
